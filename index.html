👵 Aged Care

Council Hands Off Aged Care Services to Kingston Provider AccessCare

From 1 July, a set of Commonwealth Home Support Program services — personal care, domestic assistance, respite care, escorted shopping and home maintenance — has moved from Frankston City Council to Kingston's long-running provider AccessCare, following a 2025 review. Meals on Wheels, outings and community transport stay with Council, and a new Community Care Navigation and Advocacy Service is being introduced to help residents navigate the wider aged care reforms.
